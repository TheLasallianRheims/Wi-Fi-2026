"Waltz of Four Left Feet"
ng Shirebound

- "As an avid enjoyer of OPM, this question is quite difficult to answer, but if I really had to choose one out of hundreds in my playlist, it would be Waltz of Four Left Feet by Shirebound.

Beyond its use of a simple, calming progression and gradual buildup, what I really love most about this song would be how, despite its seemingly romantic premise, its lyrics can actually resonate with experiences far beyond love. The line, “Sapat na sa ’kin ang ganito, ang pagmasdan ka sa malayo,” particularly speaks to me. Shirebound was able to put into words the feeling of having to admire something from a distance because, for one reason or another, you can no longer reach for it. For me, that something pertains to a dream, an opportunity, or even a version of my future that I once imagined for myself. Every time I listen to this song—aside from making me cry buckets—it reminds me of the things I have had to let go of and the painful realization that sometimes, no matter how much we want something, we have to learn to be content with simply knowing that it once meant something to us.

It beautifully captures the bittersweet process of accepting that some things are meant to be cherished, even if they are no longer ours. That is why this song will never cease to move me and will forever remain my all-time favorite, no matter how many times I hear it. "
